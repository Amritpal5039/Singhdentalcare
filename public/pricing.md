# Pricing & Treatments — Singh Dental Care

## General Consultations
- Price: Starting at ₹500
- Includes: Complete oral examination, X-rays (if required), and treatment planning by specialized dentists.

## Dental Implants
- Price: Starting at ₹15,000 per implant
- Features: US FDA approved implants, painless procedure, lifetime warranty on selected brands.
- Best for: Permanent replacement of missing teeth.

## Orthodontics (Braces & Clear Aligners)
- Price: Starting at ₹25,000 for braces | Clear aligners starting at ₹40,000
- Features: Invisible aligners, metal and ceramic braces options, customized treatment plans.
- Best for: Straightening misaligned teeth and correcting bite issues.

## Root Canal Therapy (RCT)
- Price: Starting at ₹3,000 per tooth
- Features: Single-visit RCT available, painless advanced endodontic treatment.
- Best for: Saving infected or decayed natural teeth.

## Cosmetic Dentistry (Teeth Whitening)
- Price: Starting at ₹5,000 per session
- Features: Instant laser teeth whitening, smile makeovers, dental veneers.
- Best for: Enhancing the aesthetic appearance of your smile.

*Note: All prices are indicative and may vary based on the specific condition and treatment plan recommended by the doctor. For precise pricing, please schedule a consultation.*
