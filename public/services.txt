## Detailed Treatments & Services

### 1. Dental Implants
- **Description**: Restore missing teeth with state-of-the-art permanent implants that mimic natural tooth roots.
- **Key benefits**: Long-lasting, fully functional, restores natural speech, preserves facial structure.
- **Best for**: Single tooth replacement, multiple teeth replacement, full-mouth reconstruction.

### 2. Orthodontics (Braces & Clear Aligners)
- **Description**: Correct misaligned teeth and jaws using advanced orthodontic techniques.
- **Offerings**: Metallic braces, ceramic braces, self-ligating braces, and premium invisible clear aligners.
- **Best for**: Crooked teeth, gap between teeth, overbite, underbite, crowded teeth.

### 3. Root Canal Therapy (RCT)
- **Description**: Relieve severe toothache and save the natural tooth from infection.
- **Process**: Painless, single-visit or multi-visit root canals using rotary technology and modern sanitization.
- **Best for**: Tooth decay reaching the pulp, fractured teeth, tooth abscess.

### 4. Pediatric Dentistry
- **Description**: Gentle and friendly dental care specifically designed for infants, children, and adolescents.
- **Services**: Kids dental checkups, cavity prevention, dental sealants, fluoride therapy, habit-breaking appliances.
- **Best for**: Pediatric oral hygiene, early detection of orthodontic issues.

### 5. Cosmetic Dentistry
- **Description**: Aesthetics and smile enhancement procedures to boost confidence.
- **Services**: Zoom teeth whitening, porcelain veneers, composite bonding, smile design, laser gum contouring.
- **Best for**: Stained or yellow teeth, chipped teeth, uneven tooth sizes, gummy smiles.

### 6. Gum Treatments (Periodontics)
- **Description**: Treatment of disease and inflammation affecting the supporting tissues of the teeth.
- **Services**: Scaling and root planing, deep cleaning, laser gum surgery, bone grafting.
- **Best for**: Bleeding gums, bad breath, loose teeth, periodontitis.

### 7. Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery
- **Description**: Specialized surgical procedures of the mouth, teeth, jaws, and face.
- **Services**: Impaction/wisdom tooth removal, dental extractions, jaw fracture management, cystic lesion removal.
- **Best for**: Impacted third molars, severe trauma, dental surgery.

### 8. Prosthodontics
- **Description**: Restoring and replacing missing teeth with prosthetic appliances.
- **Services**: Dental crowns (Zirconia, Ceramic), bridges, complete dentures, partial dentures.
- **Best for**: Restoring masticatory function, cosmetic dental rehabilitation.
